Account & Data Deletion Request

Official user data deletion portal for the AttendMate application (Developed by C. Asif)

At AttendMate, we respect your right to privacy and total ownership of your personal data. In compliance with the Google Play User Data & Account Deletion Policy, this page provides clear instructions on how you can easily delete your account, local timetable records, attendance logs, and cloud backups.

AttendMate is engineered with a local-first architecture: your timetable entries, attendance percentages, and configurations are stored in an encrypted SQLite database on your device and are never sent to proprietary third-party servers. If you synced schedules with Google Calendar or backed up data to Google Drive, you have direct, instantaneous control to remove that data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your data using either of the two official methods below:

1
Direct In-App Deletion (Immediate & Self-Serve)

If you have the AttendMate application installed on your Android device, you can delete your data immediately with zero waiting time:

STEP 1
Open AttendMate and tap on the Settings icon (top right or bottom navigation).
STEP 2
Scroll to the Google Integrations & Cloud Backup section and tap Disconnect Google Account to purge all OAuth tokens from your device Keystore.
STEP 3
Tap Delete Cloud Backup to remove the attendmate_cloud_backup.json file stored in your Google Drive.
STEP 4
Under Data Management, tap Clear All App Data → confirm deletion. This permanently wipes all subjects, timetables, and attendance logs from your device storage.
STEP 5
(Optional) You can uninstall the application. Android will purge all sandbox cache files automatically.
2
Web & Support Request (Without Reinstalling App)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, you can submit a deletion request directly to our developer support team. Requests are processed within 48 to 72 hours.

Data Retention & Deletion Specification

In accordance with Google Play developer policies, here is a breakdown of what data is permanently deleted and what temporary cache is cleared:

Data Category Specific Elements Action Taken
Timetable & Subjects Course titles, subject codes, professor names, class slot timings, and room numbers. Permanently Deleted
Attendance Logs Historical attendance records (present, absent, cancelled), margin calculations, and target quotas. Permanently Deleted
Google Drive Cloud Backup attendmate_cloud_backup.json backup snapshot stored in your user-owned Google Drive storage. Permanently Deleted
Authentication Credentials Google OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, and authentication cookies in Android Keystore. Immediately Revoked & Cleared
Temporary App Cache Local database files (attendmate.db), SharedPreferences, and temporary runtime image cache. Immediately Purged
Location & Geofences Classroom coordinates (latitude/longitude) used strictly for on-device proximity notifications. Permanently Deleted
Data Retention Period:

Because AttendMate operates on a local-first architecture and does not operate a central user database server, in-app deletion is instantaneous. For email-based requests, the deletion process and support verification is completed within 48 to 72 hours (and under no circumstance exceeds 30 calendar days). No residual copies of your data are retained on any proprietary backend servers.

How to Revoke Google Permissions Independently

You do not need to contact us or use the app to revoke AttendMate's access to your Google account. You can do so directly via Google's official security dashboard at any time:

  1. Visit your official Google Account Third-Party Connections page.
  2. Locate AttendMate in the list of authorized apps.
  3. Click Remove Access to immediately revoke all Google Calendar and Google Drive permissions.
  4. To delete backup files directly from Google Drive, open Google Drive → click Settings (gear icon) → Manage Apps → scroll to AttendMate → click Options → Delete hidden app data.
