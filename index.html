Google API Verification & Limited Use Notice

Google Calendar & Google Drive Integration

AttendMate integrates with Google Calendar and Google Drive strictly for personal timetable synchronization and secure backups. We operate on a local-first architecture and do not run proprietary backend servers that store your personal records.

Google Calendar

Used strictly to create, update, and synchronize class lecture events to your personal Google Calendar. AttendMate does not read or alter any other personal calendar events.

Google Drive

Used strictly to store and restore a single encrypted backup file (attendmate_cloud_backup.json). AttendMate has zero access to any other files or folders in your Drive.

AttendMate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell your data, never serve ads, and never use Google data to train AI models.
